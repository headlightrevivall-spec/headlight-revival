What happens to your headlights

A four-stage process refined over hundreds of restorations — no shortcuts, no drilling.

1

Inspect & assess

We check lens depth and oxidation level to confirm restoration will get you a clear, durable result.

2

Wet sand

Progressive grit sanding removes the oxidized outer layer without damaging the lens underneath.

3

Polish & buff

A machine polish brings back true optical clarity and gloss, stage by stage.

4

UV-protective seal

A clear UV sealant locks in the finish, so oxidation takes far longer to return.
